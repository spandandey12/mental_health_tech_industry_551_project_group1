# Milestone 4 Reflection

In this milestone, we finalized the development of our Workplace Mental Health Dashboard and focused on making the application production-ready. The dashboard now integrates multiple interactive visualizations that allow users to explore relationships between workplace factors and mental health treatment. We implemented filtering options for year, region, gender, age group, company size, and remote work status. These filters dynamically update all charts and KPI indicators, allowing users to explore patterns across different groups. The dashboard includes a treatment rate grouped bar chart, a chart showing work interference among respondents who sought treatment, and additional charts illustrating workplace support indicators such as benefits and help-seeking options.

One major focus of this milestone was incorporating feedback received from both our teaching assistants and peers. Based on earlier feedback, we replaced the stacked bar chart in Chart 1 with a grouped bar chart to improve interpretability when treatment rates exceed 100%. We also simplified Charts 2–4 by focusing only on respondents who reported seeking treatment, which makes the comparisons clearer and reduces unnecessary visual complexity. Additionally, we improved the overall layout and styling of the dashboard, reduced excessive white space, and ensured the charts scale properly within the page.

Some smaller improvements were also implemented to enhance usability. For example, the browser tab title was added, KPI summary cards were included to highlight key statistics, and a legend panel was added to explain variable definitions and chart encodings.

There are a few features that we initially considered but did not implement in the final version. For example, we discussed adding additional interaction controls or more complex visualizations, but we decided to prioritize performance and clarity instead. Given the size of the dataset and the scope of the project, focusing on clear and responsive visualizations was more beneficial for the user experience.

Overall, the feedback received throughout the milestones was valuable and helped guide improvements to both the visual design and analytical clarity of the dashboard. This iterative process helped us refine the application into a more usable and informative tool for exploring workplace mental health trends.
